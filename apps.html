Apps

My applications are practical tools for organizing everyday life, sustained study, and meaningful connection. They are designed to make complicated bodies of information easier to understand and act upon.

Nutrition + Fitness

Nutrition + Fitness brings food, exercise, and personal goals into one place. It is intended to support a clearer view of daily habits and long-term progress without separating nutrition from the broader context of health and physical activity.

Reading List

Reading List is a tool for building and navigating a personal program of study. It helps organize books and other texts, preserve the relationships among them, and turn an expanding collection of interests into a manageable path through one’s reading.

Brydge

Brydge is an application for interoperability among different devices. It is designed to bridge otherwise separate hardware and software environments, allowing devices to communicate and work together more easily.
