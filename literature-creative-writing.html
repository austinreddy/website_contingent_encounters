2. Star Trek: nano

A fan fiction set in the Star Trek universe, following Seven of Nine as she becomes a Starfleet captain during an interstellar crisis. When the nanobots of the Borg Collective run rampant, the Borg Queens begrudgingly request help from Starfleet and other Alpha Quadrant powers in an attempt to contain the contaminant.
