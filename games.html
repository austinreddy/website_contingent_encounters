Games

My games explore familiar worlds, speculative settings, and the strange rhythms of everyday life. Each project approaches play from a different direction, from collecting and discovery to narrative survival.

PokeLibrary

PokeLibrary is a game project centered on collecting, cataloguing, and discovering creatures and the worlds that surround them. It brings the pleasure of a personal library into conversation with the structure of a creature-collecting game.

Last Horizon

Last Horizon is a speculative game about confronting the edge of a known world. It explores distance, uncertainty, and what remains possible when the future narrows toward a final horizon.

1001 Nights of Living Hell

1001 Nights of Living Hell is a narrative game organized around endurance, repetition, and storytelling under pressure. Its title joins the proliferating tales of One Thousand and One Nights to the experience of surviving a seemingly endless ordeal.

Convenience Store

Convenience Store turns an ordinary commercial space into a setting for observation, encounter, and play. The project attends to the people, objects, and routines that briefly converge in a place designed for transience.
