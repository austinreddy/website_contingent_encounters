Games

My games explore familiar worlds, speculative settings, and the strange rhythms of everyday life. Each project approaches play from a different direction, from collecting and discovery to narrative survival.

PokeLibrary

PokeLibrary redeploys the Pokémon Red engine as a game of philosophical discovery. Instead of capturing Pokémon, the player encounters and collects philosophers together with their books, ideas, and concepts, transforming the familiar structure of a creature-collecting RPG into an explorable history of thought.

Last Horizon

Last Horizon is an online cooperative Japanese-style role-playing game. It brings players together in a shared adventure built around party-based exploration, combat, and progression.

1001 Nights of Living Hell

1001 Nights of Living Hell retells Diablo through the streets and underworld of Seattle. It transposes the original game’s descent into darkness into a contemporary urban setting, recasting its atmosphere, structure, and infernal struggle through the character of the city.

Convenience Store

Convenience Store is a GTA-style autobiographical game. It uses an open-world structure to transform personal history, everyday labor, and lived environments into spaces for narrative exploration and play.
